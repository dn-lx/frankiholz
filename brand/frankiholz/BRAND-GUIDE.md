Positioning: Accommodation stay in Frankfurt, suitable for direct-booking and Airbnb-facing use.

## Logo Usage
- Use `Logo_Teal` when the logo is placed as a complete square brand tile.
- Use `Logo_White` for documents or layouts with a white background.
- Use `Transparent_ForDark` on dark photography, teal backgrounds, or dark UI.
- Use `Transparent_ForLight` on white/light backgrounds.
- Use the icon-only version for social profile images, app icons and favicons.
- Keep clear space around the logo. Avoid stretching, skewing, recoloring, or adding extra effects.

## Digital Usage
- Website header / dark hero: Transparent_ForDark
- Website header / white navigation: Transparent_ForLight
- Social profile: Icon_Teal or Profile_1080x1080
- Open Graph / link previews: SocialShare_1200x630
- Apple touch icon: 180x180
- Android/PWA icons: 192x192 and 512x512
- Browser favicon: favicon.ico
